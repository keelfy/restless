# Changelog

## 0.1.0-alpha.1

First alpha build. Not published anywhere yet (see D5 in `docs/development-plan.md`).

- Personal and territorial spirits with a procedural type, traits, story and nature; they age, gain power and anger.
- Haunt director with 652 events and 104 scenarios, from scares to real harm.
- Investigation: spirit candle, ash, spirit box, ouija board, camera and a journal that keeps clues and photos.
- Protection: wards, salt rings, offerings, charms.
- Banishing at the altar or laying a spirit to rest at its grave; the Requiem; essences, relics and charms as rewards.
- Sanity and its screen effects.
