# Charms

Each charm is made of one essence (E), one [hallowed wax](/en/items/tools#hallowed-wax) (W, the seal) and common items, in a shape. A shape is shown as a grid, top row first, `.` an empty cell. Each charm answers to one spirit type. The [rules every charm shares](/en/items#rules-every-charm-shares) are set down apart: some are worn in an accessory slot with Curios (the off hand without), the rest are used from the hand.

## Poltergeist: Telekinesis Rod

Poltergeist essence + hallowed wax + 2 gold ingots (G), a diagonal rod:

```
..G
.W.
GE.
```

When used, it pulls every dropped item and every orb of experience around to the holder. What lies in lava stays.

## Shade: Dark Lens

Shade essence + hallowed wax + ender eye (Y) + 2 tinted glass (G):

```
G Y G
. W .
. E .
```

When used, the holder sees in the dark, living things glow through walls for a while, and the spirits nearby show where they stand as a faint dark-grey haze with a few motes of ash: easy to miss, plain once looked at closely.

## Nix: Drowned Stone

Nix essence + hallowed wax + nautilus shell (N) + 2 prismarine shards (P):

```
. N .
P W P
. E .
```

While worn on the bracelet (off hand without Curios), it grants endless breath, Dolphin's Grace and full mining speed in water, and the events of water spirits do not run on the wearer. The price is a weight, slowness, on dry ground.

## Phantom: Phantom Double

Phantom essence + hallowed wax + 3 phantom membranes (M):

```
M M M
. W .
. E .
```

When used, a faint, see-through ghost of the player stands in the player's place for 8 seconds and hostile mobs within 16 blocks go for it. It is bodiless: nothing bumps into it, and players cannot strike it, only mobs can. It wears no armor. Other players see it too. Rest: 1 minute.

## Mimic: Borrowed Face

Mimic essence + hallowed wax + 3 leather (L) + 2 string (S), a mask:

```
L L L
S W S
. E .
```

When used, for 25 seconds hostile mobs take the holder for one of their own. It ends early if the holder strikes, opens a chest or breaks a block under a hostile's eyes. Rest: 2 minutes.

## Wraith: Wraith's Breath

Wraith essence + hallowed wax + 3 cobwebs (C), a shroud:

```
C C C
. W .
. E .
```

When used, for 5 seconds the holder is a shade. Blows pass through the holder and the holder's own pass through what is struck, with no hit, knockback or hurt flash (arrows fly through too), but a fall or fire still hurts. Costs 0.2 sanity. Rest: 90 seconds.

## Mara: Borrowed Dream

Mara essence + hallowed wax + honey bottle (H) + 2 feathers (F), a pillow:

```
F H F
. W .
. E .
```

When used, sanity returns to full at once. The holder's spirits grow angrier for it. Once a day, even across a relog.

## Banshee: Death Wail

Banshee essence + hallowed wax + bell (B) + 2 string (S), a necklace:

```
S W S
. B .
. E .
```

Worn on the necklace (anywhere in the inventory without Curios). Once the wearer is below 3 hearts it screams once a day: hostile mobs within 8 blocks are thrown back, slowed and blinded for 3 seconds. It also keens 3 seconds before a spirit's assault lands.

## Revenant: Grudge

Revenant essence + hallowed wax + 3 iron ingots (I), a ring:

```
I I I
. W .
. E .
```

While worn on the ring (off hand without Curios), it stores the damage the wearer takes for 10 seconds. The wearer's next blow adds half of it, up to 10.

## Lost one: Lost Step

Lost one essence + hallowed wax + lantern (L) + compass (C):

```
. L .
C W E
```

When used, the holder slips up to 8 blocks ahead, through a wall of a block or two, but not through obsidian, iron doors or bars, or bedrock. The holder comes out where there is room. Costs 0.1 sanity. Rest: 20 seconds.

## Domovoi: House Key

Domovoi essence + hallowed wax + 2 copper ingots (C), a key:

```
W C
. C
. E
```

While worn on the belt (off hand without Curios), inside a shut, roofed room, it keeps the events of spirits from running. Each one stopped wears the key. It has 16 uses. Domovoi's Porridge mends it by 8 in an anvil.

## Hoarder: Stash

Hoarder essence + hallowed wax + any bundle + emerald, shapeless. Holds one stack. When used, it opens its slot: a stack can be put in or taken out. It will not hold another stash or satchel. Kept anywhere in the inventory at the owner's death, the stack stays with the owner, even through a server stop, and the stash is spent. It does not work with `keepInventory`.

## Demon: Bargain

Demon essence + hallowed wax + blaze rod (R) + 2 gold ingots (G):

```
G R G
. W .
. E .
```

When used, it restores all health at once and grants 30 seconds of strength II, resistance II and fire resistance. Then comes 10 minutes with two hearts fewer and angrier spirits. It will not strike a second bargain while the first is unpaid.

## Trickster: Trickster's Coin

Trickster essence + hallowed wax + 7 gold nuggets (N), a coin:

```
N N N
N E N
N W N
```

When used, the holder wagers the next mob kill for 10 minutes. When the mob dies, its drops and experience are doubled or lost, evenly. Each win in a row pays one more, up to x5; a loss starts over. A streak fades by one a day. A win bursts gold sparks from the mob (more with each streak step) with a chime; a loss leaves grey smoke and a hiss. Rest: 1 minute.
