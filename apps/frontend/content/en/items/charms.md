# Charms

One essence and one common item, crafted together, shapeless. Each charm answers to one spirit type. Read the [rules every charm shares](/en/items#rules-every-charm-shares) first: most work from the off hand, one at a time.

## Poltergeist: Telekinesis Amulet

Poltergeist essence + gold ingot + string. In the off hand it pulls every dropped item and every orb of experience around to you. What lies in lava stays.

## Shade: Dark Eye

Shade essence + ender eye. In the off hand you see in the dark, living things glow through walls for a while, and the spirits around you are marked by columns of soul fire.

## Nix: Drowned Stone

Nix essence + nautilus shell. In the off hand: endless breath, Dolphin's Grace and full mining speed in water, and the events of water spirits do not run on you. The price is a weight, slowness, on dry ground.

## Phantom: Phantom Double

Phantom essence + phantom membrane. Use it: a faint, see-through ghost of you stands where you are for 8 seconds and hostile mobs within 16 blocks go for it. It is bodiless: nothing bumps into it, and players cannot strike it, only mobs can. It wears no armor. Other players see it too. Rest: 1 minute.

## Mimic: Borrowed Face

Mimic essence + leather. Use it: for 25 seconds hostile mobs take you for one of their own. It ends early if you strike, or open a chest or break a block under a hostile's eyes. Rest: 2 minutes.

## Wraith: Wraith Shroud

Wraith essence + iron ingot. Use it: for 5 seconds you are a shade. Blows pass through you and yours pass through what you strike, with no hit, knockback or hurt flash (arrows fly through too), but a fall or fire still hurts. Costs 0.2 sanity. Rest: 90 seconds.

## Mara: Borrowed Dream

Mara essence + honey bottle. Use it: sanity back to full at once. Your spirits grow angrier for it. Once a day, even across a relog.

## Banshee: Death Wail

Banshee essence + bell. Carried anywhere in the inventory. Below 3 hearts it screams once a day: hostile mobs within 8 blocks are thrown back, slowed and blinded for 3 seconds. It also keens 3 seconds before a spirit's assault lands.

## Revenant: Grudge

Revenant essence + iron sword. In the off hand it stores the damage you take for 10 seconds. Your next blow adds half of it, up to 10.

## Lost one: Lost Step

Lost one essence + lantern. Use it: slip up to 8 blocks ahead, through a wall of a block or two, but not through obsidian, iron doors or bars, or bedrock. You come out where there is room. Costs 0.1 sanity. Rest: 20 seconds.

## Domovoi: House Key

Domovoi essence + campfire. In the off hand, inside a shut, roofed room: the events of spirits do not run. Each one stopped wears the key. It has 16 uses. Domovoi's Porridge mends it by 8 in an anvil.

## Hoarder: Stash

Hoarder essence + emerald. Holds one stack. Use it with something in the other hand and it takes that in; use it full and it gives the stack back. Anywhere in your inventory when you die, the stack stays with you, even through a server stop, and the stash is spent. It does not work with `keepInventory`.

## Demon: Bargain

Demon essence + blaze powder. Use it: all your health at once, and 30 seconds of strength II, resistance II and fire resistance. Then 10 minutes with two hearts fewer and angrier spirits. It will not strike a second bargain while the first is unpaid.

## Trickster: Trickster's Coin

Trickster essence + gold nugget. Use it: you wager your next mob kill for 10 minutes. When it dies, its drops and experience are doubled or lost, evenly. Each win in a row pays one more, up to x5; a loss starts over. A streak fades by one a day. A win bursts gold sparks from the mob (more with each streak step) with a chime; a loss leaves grey smoke and a hiss. Rest: 1 minute.
