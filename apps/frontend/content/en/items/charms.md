# Charms

Each charm is made of one essence (E), one [hallowed wax](/en/items/tools#hallowed-wax) (W, the seal) and common items, in a shape. A shape is shown as a grid, top row first, `.` an empty cell. Each charm answers to one spirit type. Read the [rules every charm shares](/en/items#rules-every-charm-shares) first: some are worn in an accessory slot with Curios (the off hand without), the rest are used from the hand.

## Poltergeist: Telekinesis Rod

Poltergeist essence + hallowed wax + 2 gold ingots (G), a diagonal rod:

```
..G
.W.
GE.
```

Use it: it pulls every dropped item and every orb of experience around to you. What lies in lava stays.

## Shade: Dark Lens

Shade essence + hallowed wax + ender eye (Y) + 2 tinted glass (G):

```
G Y G
. W .
. E .
```

Use it: you see in the dark, living things glow through walls for a while, and the spirits around you show where they stand as a faint dark-grey haze with a few motes of ash: easy to miss, plain once you look.

## Nix: Drowned Stone

Nix essence + hallowed wax + nautilus shell (N) + 2 prismarine shards (P):

```
. N .
P W P
. E .
```

Worn on the bracelet (off hand without Curios): endless breath, Dolphin's Grace and full mining speed in water, and the events of water spirits do not run on you. The price is a weight, slowness, on dry ground.

## Phantom: Phantom Double

Phantom essence + hallowed wax + 3 phantom membranes (M):

```
M M M
. W .
. E .
```

Use it: a faint, see-through ghost of you stands where you are for 8 seconds and hostile mobs within 16 blocks go for it. It is bodiless: nothing bumps into it, and players cannot strike it, only mobs can. It wears no armor. Other players see it too. Rest: 1 minute.

## Mimic: Borrowed Face

Mimic essence + hallowed wax + 3 leather (L) + 2 string (S), a mask:

```
L L L
S W S
. E .
```

Use it: for 25 seconds hostile mobs take you for one of their own. It ends early if you strike, or open a chest or break a block under a hostile's eyes. Rest: 2 minutes.

## Wraith: Wraith's Breath

Wraith essence + hallowed wax + 3 cobwebs (C), a shroud:

```
C C C
. W .
. E .
```

Use it: for 5 seconds you are a shade. Blows pass through you and yours pass through what you strike, with no hit, knockback or hurt flash (arrows fly through too), but a fall or fire still hurts. Costs 0.2 sanity. Rest: 90 seconds.

## Mara: Borrowed Dream

Mara essence + hallowed wax + honey bottle (H) + 2 feathers (F), a pillow:

```
F H F
. W .
. E .
```

Use it: sanity back to full at once. Your spirits grow angrier for it. Once a day, even across a relog.

## Banshee: Death Wail

Banshee essence + hallowed wax + bell (B) + 2 string (S), a necklace:

```
S W S
. B .
. E .
```

Worn on the necklace (anywhere in the inventory without Curios). Below 3 hearts it screams once a day: hostile mobs within 8 blocks are thrown back, slowed and blinded for 3 seconds. It also keens 3 seconds before a spirit's assault lands.

## Revenant: Grudge

Revenant essence + hallowed wax + 3 iron ingots (I), a ring:

```
I I I
. W .
. E .
```

Worn on the ring (off hand without Curios), it stores the damage you take for 10 seconds. Your next blow adds half of it, up to 10.

## Lost one: Lost Step

Lost one essence + hallowed wax + lantern (L) + compass (C):

```
. L .
C W E
```

Use it: slip up to 8 blocks ahead, through a wall of a block or two, but not through obsidian, iron doors or bars, or bedrock. You come out where there is room. Costs 0.1 sanity. Rest: 20 seconds.

## Domovoi: House Key

Domovoi essence + hallowed wax + 2 copper ingots (C), a key:

```
W C
. C
. E
```

Worn on the belt (off hand without Curios), inside a shut, roofed room: the events of spirits do not run. Each one stopped wears the key. It has 16 uses. Domovoi's Porridge mends it by 8 in an anvil.

## Hoarder: Stash

Hoarder essence + hallowed wax + any bundle + emerald, shapeless. Holds one stack. Right-click it to open its slot: put a stack in, or take it out. It will not hold another stash or satchel. Anywhere in your inventory when you die, the stack stays with you, even through a server stop, and the stash is spent. It does not work with `keepInventory`.

## Demon: Bargain

Demon essence + hallowed wax + blaze rod (R) + 2 gold ingots (G):

```
G R G
. W .
. E .
```

Use it: all your health at once, and 30 seconds of strength II, resistance II and fire resistance. Then 10 minutes with two hearts fewer and angrier spirits. It will not strike a second bargain while the first is unpaid.

## Trickster: Trickster's Coin

Trickster essence + hallowed wax + 7 gold nuggets (N), a coin:

```
N N N
N E N
N W N
```

Use it: you wager your next mob kill for 10 minutes. When it dies, its drops and experience are doubled or lost, evenly. Each win in a row pays one more, up to x5; a loss starts over. A streak fades by one a day. A win bursts gold sparks from the mob (more with each streak step) with a chime; a loss leaves grey smoke and a hiss. Rest: 1 minute.
