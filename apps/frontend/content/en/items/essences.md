# Essences

Each of the fifteen spirit types leaves one kind of essence, named for it: poltergeist, shade, nix, phantom, mimic, wraith, mara, banshee, revenant, lost one, domovoi, hoarder, demon, trickster and boggart.

## Where they come from

| Source | Essences |
| --- | --- |
| Banishing a spirit at an altar | 1 |
| Banishing a spirit that had **evolved** | +1 (its ritual is longer, by 4 waves) |
| Banishing a territorial spirit whose blight had grown to **64 spots** | +1 (a reward for fighting one that was left to spread) |
| Putting a grave right | 2 |

The banishing is the altar ritual: an altar inside a closed salt loop, at least 4 lit candles within 3 blocks, and an offering the spirit is weak to. The ritual runs 12 waves of 5 seconds; a form the spirit's type does not take adds 6 waves. The spirit fights back through every wave and snuffs candles. If every candle is lit at the end, it is banished.

## Calling a type on purpose

Some types are hard to meet by chance. Two ways to meet them:

### The effigy

A scarecrow stuffed with one essence, lit with flint and steel or a fire charge. It burns for half a night (12000 ticks) and then calls a spirit of that type.

- **Craft:** scarecrow + candle + salt, shapeless. The scarecrow is hay block + carved pumpkin + stick + salt.
- **Stuff it:** use an essence on it. Breaking it before it is lit gives the essence back.
- **Personal type:** born on the nearest player within 24 blocks of the effigy.
- **Territorial type:** anchored where the effigy stood.
- **It will not light** when the call could not be answered: the newest spirit is too young, the player already has all they can carry, or another territorial spirit is close. No essence is wasted.

### Deeds and places

A spirit is born when a deed is done or a structure is found, by chance and after a cooldown (3 days between births for one player; no more than 3 personal spirits at a time). A new spirit starts at anger 0.5 and gives two minutes of grace. Territorial spirits keep 96 blocks apart.

**Personal, by deed**

| Spirit | The deed | Chance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Revenant | a slaughter: 24 points of kills within three minutes (a villager is worth 5) | 0.3 |
| Revenant | killing your own tamed pet | 0.5 |
| Lost one | dying 256 or more blocks from your spawn | 0.5 |
| Lost one | two days awake: no bed slept in | 0.3 |
| Banshee | a tamed pet dying away from you | 0.5 |
| Banshee | a villager or tamed animal dying within 16 blocks, not by your hand | 0.2 |

**Territorial, by structure**

| Spirit | Structure | Chance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Poltergeist | woodland mansion | 0.6 |
| Poltergeist | trail ruins | 0.3 |
| Nix | shipwreck, ocean ruin | 0.25 |
| Demon | ancient city | 0.5 |
| Demon | ruined portal, bastion remnant, nether fortress | 0.2 |
| Trickster | swamp hut | 0.5 |
| Hoarder | igloo, desert pyramid, jungle pyramid | 0.3 |
| Domovoi | village | 0.1 |

The journal's page for a type says where it is met once you have identified one.

## What to spend them on

- One essence and a common item makes a [charm](/en/items/charms).
- A charm and the essence of another type makes an [advanced charm](/en/items/advanced).
- Three essences of different types and a rare find make a [relic](/en/items/relics).
- One essence and common items makes several [consumables](/en/items/consumables).
- One essence lights an effigy.
