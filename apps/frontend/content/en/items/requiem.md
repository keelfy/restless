# The Requiem

The finale. A spirit is a dead person with unfinished business; the Requiem is yours. You carry one essence of every type to the altar and sing the dead out. A player who completes it is **released**: no spirit is born on them or acts on them again.

## The Reliquary

A vessel for the essences, crafted early from an amethyst shard, a gold block, salt and a book, once the essence chapter of the journal is open. It is the visible progress.

- **Fill it:** use an essence with the reliquary in the other hand. It holds **one essence of each of the fifteen types**, one slot per type, and refuses a second of the same type.
- **Named types only:** an essence of a type you have never photographed is refused. The type has to have been met and named in the journal.
- **Tooltip:** lists the types held and greys out the missing ones; a missing type that the journal has named shows its "where met" line.
- **Full, it glows.** Only a full reliquary is taken by the altar.
- **The order matters:** the ritual's movements follow the order the essences went in.

## The ritual

Offer the full reliquary at an [altar](/en/items/tools#altar) with:

- a closed ring of salt around it,
- **14 lit candles** within 4 blocks,
- a **bell** within 8 blocks.

The altar refuses if you are already released, if a ritual is already running, or while the Requiem is wary (see Failure). The reliquary stays in your inventory; it is not consumed. The rite takes about **6 minutes** in **14 movements**, one per type, each a short test of the counter you learned against that type. The progress shows on the action bar: the movement's name and the candles still lit.

**The candles are the life bar.** A failed movement snuffs one candle. When the last movement ends, fewer than **7 lit** means the Requiem has failed.

| Type | Movement (about 25 s) | Passed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shade | The light dies around the altar | at least 4 light sources still lit at the end (Hallowed Lanterns count) |
| Poltergeist | Items fly from your inventory | picking all of them back up (the Telekinesis Rod helps) |
| Nix | Water rises in the circle | not drowning, and keeping the altar dry (the Drowned Stone helps) |
| Phantom | Six strangers stand outside the ring | not looking at any of them for the whole movement |
| Mimic | Two false altars appear | putting holy water on the real one |
| Wraith | Darkness, blows from nowhere | staying above 3 hearts; light under 4 doubles the damage |
| Mara | You fall asleep standing | waking before it ends: sanity above 0.3 when it starts |
| Banshee | A wail, then silence | ringing the bell during the silence |
| Revenant | Mobs from your past kills rise | killing them all |
| Lost one | You are moved 30 blocks away | walking back into the ring in time (Lost Step and Wayfinder's Ash help) |
| Domovoi | Doors near the altar slam | closing every door within 8 blocks |
| Hoarder | The offering is stolen: a token is hidden in one of 3 chests nearby | finding it and carrying it back (the Magpie Feather points at the chests) |
| Demon | A bargain is offered in chat, with Accept and Refuse buttons | refusing it; accepting skips the movement but costs 2 candles |
| Trickster | Candles are snuffed and decoys appear, colors swapped | relighting the ones it snuffed, not the decoys |

Charms and consumables are fair to use: a movement one of them helps is the point of having collected it. They are used as you would use them anywhere; the rite counts only what the movement checks.

Dying, logging out, leaving the dimension or stopping the server ends the rite as a failure **without** the loss of an essence and without the wary rest. Everything it changed is put back.

## Failure

- The reliquary **keeps its essences except one, lost at random**.
- Every personal spirit gains **0.3 anger**.
- The altar refuses a Requiem for **3 in-game days**.

## Success

- Each personal spirit is **laid to rest**: it leaves a gravestone with a waxed sign that names it, and its journal dossier is closed with a last line.
- You become **released**.
- The reliquary becomes the **Requiem Bell**.
- You get the advancement, the journal opens a closing chapter, and a short closing text plays.

## Released

| System | For a released player |
| --- | --- |
| Births (deeds, effigies, `/haunt spirit add` without force) | nothing is born |
| Personal spirits | none; existing ones were laid to rest |
| Territorial spirits | do not act on you; still act on others, still blight the land, and can still be banished by you (and drop essence) |
| Sanity | held at full, the overlay off |
| Charms | all work |
| Journal | a closing chapter; the dossiers stay readable |

Released is a saved state, not an item, so dying does not lose it. Other players are never affected: the Requiem is personal.

## Going back

Sneak and ring the **Requiem Bell** for 5 seconds to give the release back. Three fresh personal spirits are born at the start anger, and the Bell stays. A second Requiem needs a new reliquary.
