# Advanced charms

Put a charm and one essence of **another** type in a crafting grid, shapeless. The result is an advanced charm that does what its base did, and more or differently. It keeps what the base held: a stash's stack, a rest not yet over. They are used the same way as the base: worn in the slot named on each, or used from the hand. See [where a worn charm goes](/en/items#rules-every-charm-shares).

## Fetch's Path

The Lost Step, and a double of you stays where the step began for 4 seconds. Hostile mobs within 16 blocks go for it, so you leave them behind. Costs 0.15 sanity in all, and rests 25 seconds.

## Changeling Guise

The Borrowed Face, but the first blow does not break it. The mob you struck is **framed**, and the nearest hostile mob turns on it. The second blow breaks the face as before.

## Nightwalk Shroud

Worn on the back it is on: no toggle. Without Curios it is a toggle: use it to put it on, again to take it off. While it is on and the light at your feet is 4 or lower, hostile mobs lose track of you, and drop a target they had. It costs 0.01 sanity a second. Light of 5 or more ends it, and so does taking the shroud off, putting it away or a server stop; worn on the back it starts again by itself in the dark. While it is on you also pass through mobs: nothing shoves you and you shove nothing.

## Blood Debt

The Grudge with no cap on the stored blow. If the blow kills, you **heal** what was stored. Every blow costs 0.05 sanity. Worn on the ring, like the Grudge.

## Requital Wail

The Death Wail, and those it throws back glow and take half again from all damage for 10 seconds. Worn on the necklace, like the wail.

## Tidecaller

Everything the Drowned Stone does, including the weight on dry ground, and in water or rain a riptide-like dash when used. No dash on dry ground. Rest: 3 seconds. Worn on the bracelet, like the stone.

## Magpie's Hook

The Telekinesis Rod, but only items within 6 blocks come to you. Sneak-use it on a container to **link** it: what does not fit in your inventory goes into that container instead, within 64 blocks. The link breaks with the container. Worn on the hands (off hand without Curios).

## Truthseer's Eye

The Dark Lens. Once a day the next voice or planchette reading cannot lie. It is spent only when the spirit you read has a lying trait, so a reading of an honest spirit costs nothing. Worn on the head (off hand without Curios).

## Hearth Dream

The Borrowed Dream with the anger taken out: sanity to full, and your spirits do not grow angrier. It works only in a shut, roofed room. Once a day.

## Keeper's Key

The House Key, bound to the first room it shelters. There it **never wears**, within 12 blocks of that spot while the room is shut and roofed. Elsewhere it does nothing. Worn on the belt. Craft it with a campfire to unbind it and bind it again.

## Grave Satchel

The Stash for three stacks. Use it with something in the other hand to add that stack (up to three); use it with nothing in the other hand, or when it is full, and all the stacks come back at once. Worn on the belt (off hand without Curios) when you die, the whole satchel stays with you, full, even through a server stop, and rests for three days before it can be used again.

## Devil's Coin

The Trickster's Coin, but the wager stands for every kill for 30 seconds, each settled on its own. A loss ends it. Each toss raises your spirits' anger a little.

## Twin's Mirror

The Phantom Double, but the double stands **where you look**, up to 16 blocks away, and holds what you hold. Other players see it too.

## Black Bargain

The Bargain with a longer fury, 45 seconds, and the debt is 0.4 sanity at once instead of two hearts. Rests 10 minutes.
