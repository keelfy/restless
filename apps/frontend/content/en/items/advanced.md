# Advanced charms

A charm, one essence of **another** type and a **catalyst** go into a crafting grid, shapeless. The catalyst is a rare vanilla find, listed with each. The result is an advanced charm that does what its base did, and more or differently. It keeps what the base held: a stash's stack, a rest not yet over. It is used the same way as the base: worn in the slot named on each, or used from the hand. [Where a worn charm goes](/en/items#rules-every-charm-shares) is set down apart.

## Fetch's Path

The Lost Step, and a double of the holder stays where the step began for 4 seconds. Hostile mobs within 16 blocks go for it, so the holder leaves them behind. Costs 0.15 sanity in all, and rests 25 seconds.

## Changeling Guise

The Borrowed Face, made with boggart essence, but the first blow does not break it. The mob struck is **framed**, and the nearest hostile mob turns on it. The second blow breaks the face as before.

## Nightwalk Shroud

While worn on the back it is on: no toggle. Without Curios it is a toggle: when used it is put on, and when used again it is taken off. While it is on and the light at the wearer's feet is 4 or lower, hostile mobs lose track of the wearer, and drop a target they had. It costs 0.01 sanity a second. Light of 5 or more ends it, and so does taking the shroud off, putting it away or a server stop; worn on the back it starts again by itself in the dark. While it is on the wearer also passes through mobs: nothing shoves the wearer and the wearer shoves nothing.

## Blood Debt

The Grudge with no cap on the stored blow. If the blow kills, the wearer **heals** what was stored. Every blow costs 0.05 sanity. Worn on the ring, like the Grudge.

## Requital Wail

The Death Wail, and those it throws back glow and take half again from all damage for 10 seconds. Worn on the necklace, like the wail.

## Tidecaller

Everything the Drowned Stone does, including the weight on dry ground, and in water or rain a riptide-like dash when used. No dash on dry ground. Rest: 3 seconds. Worn on the bracelet, like the stone.

## Magpie's Hook

The Telekinesis Rod, but only items within 6 blocks come to the holder. When used on a container while sneaking, it **links** that container: what does not fit in the inventory goes into the container instead, within 64 blocks. The link breaks with the container. Worn on the hands (off hand without Curios).

## Truthseer's Eye

The Dark Lens. Once a day the next voice or planchette reading cannot lie. It is spent only when the spirit being read has a lying trait, so a reading of an honest spirit costs nothing. Worn on the head (off hand without Curios).

## Hearth Dream

The Borrowed Dream with the anger taken out: sanity to full, and the holder's spirits do not grow angrier. It works only in a shut, roofed room. Once a day.

## Keeper's Key

The House Key, bound to the first room it shelters. There it **never wears**, within 12 blocks of that spot while the room is shut and roofed. Elsewhere it does nothing. Worn on the belt. Crafted again with a campfire, it is unbound and bound anew.

## Grave Satchel

The Stash for three stacks. When used, it opens its three slots: stacks can be put in or taken out. It will not hold a stash or another satchel. Worn on the belt (off hand without Curios) at the owner's death, the whole satchel stays with the owner, full, even through a server stop, and rests for three days before it can be used again.

## Devil's Coin

The Trickster's Coin, but the wager stands for every kill for 30 seconds, each settled on its own. A loss ends it. Each toss raises the anger of the holder's spirits a little.

## Twin's Mirror

The Phantom Double, but the double stands **where the holder looks**, up to 16 blocks away, and holds what the holder holds. Other players see it too.

## Black Bargain

The Bargain with a longer fury, 45 seconds, and the debt is 0.4 sanity at once instead of two hearts. Rests 10 minutes.
