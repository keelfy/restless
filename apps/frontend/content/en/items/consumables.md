# Consumables

Each essence also makes something that is spent as it is used. They are crafted in a crafting grid, shapeless, from the essence and common items, and one craft makes several. Times are in game time; a day is twenty minutes.

## Poltergeist: Restless Knot

Poltergeist essence + string + 2 iron chains makes **4**. Tie it on a container: for 10 minutes the container takes in items dropped within 8 blocks, like a hopper with no pipe. A full container lets them lie. One knot to a container.

## Shade: Dusk Salt

Shade essence + salt + coal makes **4**. Scatter it on a block: for a day no hostile mob spawns of itself within 12 blocks. Spawners and spirits' events are not stopped.

## Nix: Still Water

Nix essence + water bottle + prismarine shard makes **2**. Drink it: the rain and thunder clear, and the sky is held for a day. Drink it sneaking: a storm for 10 minutes. Overworld only.

## Phantom: Sighting Mark

Phantom essence + paper + glowstone dust makes **4**. Set it on a block: a pale figure glows through walls there, where you can see it from afar, for three days. Rename the mark to name the figure. Eight at most at a time.

## Mimic: Mimic Clay

Mimic essence + 4 clay balls makes **8**. Press it on a block and it takes that block's look. Set down, it is a solid block that looks like the original and breaks as slowly. The Dark Eye shows it bare. Breaking it gives the clay back, with its look.

## Wraith: Wraith Oil

Wraith essence + glass bottle + ink sac makes **2**. Use it with a weapon in the other hand: 30 blows that, in light 7 or lower, hit half again as hard through armor.

## Mara: Dreamless Draught

Mara essence + honey bottle + poppy makes **3**. Drink it before bed: you may sleep with monsters near, and the night passes with only you in bed. You wake with 0.3 sanity more, and your mara does not act that night.

## Banshee: Omen Candle

Banshee essence + candle + bone meal makes **4**. Light it with fire; it burns for a day. A harmful event on a player within 16 blocks is announced by its wail 5 seconds before it lands. The lights events snuff it, as they do any candle.

## Revenant: Bloodied Rag

Revenant essence + 2 rotten flesh + red dye makes **4**. Throw it at a mob: for 15 seconds every mob of its type within 24 blocks goes for it. Bosses are not moved.

## Lost one: Wayfinder's Ash

Lost one essence + ash powder + bone makes **4**. Use it: footprints lead to where you last died, for 3 minutes. Use it sneaking and they lead to your respawn point. One trail at a time.

## Domovoi: Domovoi's Porridge

Domovoi essence + bowl + wheat + milk bucket makes **4**. Set it in a shut, roofed room: the house spirit eats it over a day. For that day crops and saplings within 16 blocks grow three times as fast, and young animals grow up sooner and breed again sooner. One bowl to a room. In an anvil it mends a House Key by 8 uses.

## Hoarder: Magpie Feather

Hoarder essence + feather + gold nugget makes **4**. Use it: for 2 minutes a stream of light leads to the nearest chest, barrel or pot nobody has opened.

## Demon: Infernal Contract

Demon essence + paper + blaze powder makes **1**. Use it with an enchanted item in the other hand: one enchantment that stands at its highest level goes one past it. The item takes the Curse of Vanishing and your spirits grow angrier. Once per item.

## Trickster: Changeling Dust

Trickster essence + gold nugget + redstone makes **4**. Use it on a block to turn it into another of its family: wool, carpet, logs, planks, stone variants, concrete, glass, terracotta, glazed terracotta. Use it on a villager who never traded to deal their trades again.
