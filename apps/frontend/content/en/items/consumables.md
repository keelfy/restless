# Consumables

From each essence I also learned to make something that is spent as it is used. These are crafted in a crafting grid, shapeless, from the essence and common items, and one craft makes several. The yield rule: **4** by default; a drink makes one per vessel in the recipe (bottles, honey bottles); the Infernal Contract makes 1; Mimic Clay makes 8. No wax is needed. Times are in game time; a day is twenty minutes.

## Poltergeist: Restless Knot

Poltergeist essence + string + 2 iron chains makes **4**. Tied on a container, the knot takes in items dropped within 8 blocks, like a hopper with no pipe, until it is taken back. When used sneaking on the tied container, the knot comes off. If the container is broken, the knot drops. A full container lets items lie. One knot to a container.

## Shade: Dusk Salt

Shade essence + 2 salt + ash makes **4**. Scattered on a block, the salt keeps any hostile mob from spawning of itself within 12 blocks, until it is swept up. When used sneaking within 2 blocks of the spot, the salt is taken back. Spawners and spirits' events are not stopped.

## Nix: Still Water

Nix essence + 2 [holy water](/en/items/tools#holy-water) + prismarine shard makes **2**. When drunk, it clears the rain and thunder, and the weather then runs its own course. When drunk sneaking, it brings a storm of the length a natural one has. Overworld only.

## Phantom: Sighting Mark

Phantom essence + paper + glowstone dust makes **4**. Set on a block, the mark marks the spot with a waypoint until it is taken up again. Its icon shows through walls on the screen of every player who has it in view, from afar, and keeps one small, slightly see-through size however far it is, with the distance beneath. When the player interacts with the mark by hand, it is taken back. A renamed mark carries its new name. Eight at most at a time.

## Mimic: Mimic Clay

Mimic essence + 4 clay balls makes **8**. Pressed on a block, the clay takes that block's look. Set down, it is a solid block that looks like the original and breaks as slowly. It turns the way the block it copies would turn if set down just so: a log lies along the face it is set against, stairs face the player. The Dark Lens shows it bare. Broken, it gives the clay back, with its look.

## Wraith: Wraith Oil

Wraith essence + 2 glass bottles + ink sac makes **2**. When used with a weapon in the other hand, it grants 30 blows that, in light 7 or lower, hit half again as hard through armor.

## Mara: Dreamless Draught

Mara essence + 3 honey bottles + poppy makes **3**. When drunk before bed, it allows sleep with monsters near, and the night passes with only the player in bed. The sleeper wakes with 0.3 sanity more, and their mara does not act that night.

## Banshee: Omen Candle

Banshee essence + 4 candles + bone meal makes **4**. Lit with fire, it burns until it has served. A harmful event on a player within 16 blocks is announced by its wail 5 seconds before it lands, and the candle goes out when the event does. It is lit again for the next. The lights events snuff it, as they do any candle.

## Revenant: Bloodied Rag

Revenant essence + 2 rotten flesh + red dye makes **4**. Thrown at a mob, the rag sends every mob of its type within 24 blocks after it until it dies, and it glows. Bosses are not moved. A restart of the world ends the smear.

## Lost one: Wayfinder's Ash

Lost one essence + ash + bone makes **4**. When used, footprints lead to where the player last died, for 5 minutes. When used sneaking, they lead to the respawn point. One trail at a time.

## Domovoi: Domovoi's Porridge

Domovoi essence + 4 bowls + wheat + milk bucket makes **4**. Whoever eats it gains Growth, which has no end of its own, only death or a bucket of milk. While it lasts, crops and saplings within 32 blocks of the eater grow three times as fast, and young animals grow up sooner and breed again sooner. The bowl is left behind. In an anvil the porridge mends a House Key by 8 uses.

## Hoarder: Magpie Feather

Hoarder essence + feather + gold nugget makes **4**. When used, for 5 minutes, or until somebody opens it, a stream of light leads to the nearest chest or barrel with loot nobody has opened. Only chests and barrels with a loot table count: not pots.

## Demon: Infernal Contract

Demon essence + paper + blaze powder makes **1**. When used with an enchanted item in the other hand, one enchantment that stands at its highest level goes one past it. The item takes the Curse of Vanishing and the holder's spirits grow angrier. Once per item.

## Trickster: Changeling Dust

Trickster essence + gold nugget + redstone makes **4**. When used on a block, it turns that block, and every block of the same kind joined to it (up to 64), into one other of its family: wool, carpet, logs, planks, stone variants, concrete, glass, terracotta, glazed terracotta. When used on a novice villager, traded with or not, it deals their trades again. A villager who has grown past novice is left as they are.
