# Consumables

Each essence also makes something that is spent as it is used. They are crafted in a crafting grid, shapeless, from the essence and common items, and one craft makes several. Times are in game time; a day is twenty minutes.

## Poltergeist: Restless Knot

Poltergeist essence + string + 2 iron chains makes **4**. Tie it on a container: it takes in items dropped within 8 blocks, like a hopper with no pipe, until you take the knot back. Use a knot sneaking on the tied container and it comes off. If the container is broken, the knot drops. A full container lets items lie. One knot to a container.

## Shade: Dusk Salt

Shade essence + salt + coal makes **4**. Scatter it on a block: no hostile mob spawns of itself within 12 blocks, until you sweep the salt up. Use salt sneaking within 2 blocks of the spot and you take it back. Spawners and spirits' events are not stopped.

## Nix: Still Water

Nix essence + water bottle + prismarine shard makes **2**. Drink it: the rain and thunder clear, and the weather then runs its own course. Drink it sneaking: a storm for 10 minutes. Overworld only.

## Phantom: Sighting Mark

Phantom essence + paper + glowstone dust makes **4**. Set it on a block: a waypoint marks the spot for three days. Its icon shows through walls on the screen of every player who has it in view, from afar, and keeps one size however far it is, with the distance beneath. Rename the mark to name it. Eight at most at a time.

## Mimic: Mimic Clay

Mimic essence + 4 clay balls makes **8**. Press it on a block and it takes that block's look. Set down, it is a solid block that looks like the original and breaks as slowly. It turns the way the block it copies would turn if you set it down just so: a log lies along the face you click, stairs face you. The Dark Eye shows it bare. Breaking it gives the clay back, with its look.

## Wraith: Wraith Oil

Wraith essence + glass bottle + ink sac makes **2**. Use it with a weapon in the other hand: 30 blows that, in light 7 or lower, hit half again as hard through armor.

## Mara: Dreamless Draught

Mara essence + honey bottle + poppy makes **3**. Drink it before bed: you may sleep with monsters near, and the night passes with only you in bed. You wake with 0.3 sanity more, and your mara does not act that night.

## Banshee: Omen Candle

Banshee essence + candle + bone meal makes **4**. Light it with fire; it burns until it has served. A harmful event on a player within 16 blocks is announced by its wail 5 seconds before it lands, and the candle goes out when the event does. Light it again for the next. The lights events snuff it, as they do any candle.

## Revenant: Bloodied Rag

Revenant essence + 2 rotten flesh + red dye makes **4**. Throw it at a mob: until that mob dies, every mob of its type within 24 blocks goes for it, and it glows. Bosses are not moved. A restart of the world ends the smear.

## Lost one: Wayfinder's Ash

Lost one essence + ash powder + bone makes **4**. Use it: footprints lead to where you last died, for 5 minutes. Use it sneaking and they lead to your respawn point. One trail at a time.

## Domovoi: Domovoi's Porridge

Domovoi essence + bowl + wheat + milk bucket makes **4**. Eat it: you gain Growth, which has no end of its own, only your death or a bucket of milk. While it lasts, crops and saplings within 16 blocks of you grow three times as fast, and young animals grow up sooner and breed again sooner. You leave the bowl. In an anvil the porridge mends a House Key by 8 uses.

## Hoarder: Magpie Feather

Hoarder essence + feather + gold nugget makes **4**. Use it: for 5 minutes, or until somebody opens it, a stream of light leads to the nearest chest with loot nobody has opened. Only chests with a loot table count: not barrels or pots.

## Demon: Infernal Contract

Demon essence + paper + blaze powder makes **1**. Use it with an enchanted item in the other hand: one enchantment that stands at its highest level goes one past it. The item takes the Curse of Vanishing and your spirits grow angrier. Once per item.

## Trickster: Changeling Dust

Trickster essence + gold nugget + redstone makes **4**. Use it on a block to turn it, and every block of the same kind joined to it (up to 64), into one other of its family: wool, carpet, logs, planks, stone variants, concrete, glass, terracotta, glazed terracotta. Use it on a novice villager, traded with or not, to deal their trades again. A villager who has grown past novice is left as they are.
