# Tells

24 small signs, one per habit, that only a spirit with that habit gives. Each is a light event with low impact and never harms. Whatever the player sees, the journal notes it ("Seen: ...") against that spirit, so watching is a way to learn which habits it has.

Above anger 0.7, four of them turn harsh and run a harmful event instead: `tell_door_slammer` locks a door (`locked_door`), `tell_cold_touch` brings frostbite (`frostbite`), `tell_vengeful` strikes (`wrath`), `tell_whisperer` drains sanity (`sanity_drain`). See [Anger](/en/how-it-works#anger).

## `tell_cold_touch`

Only a spirit with the cold touch habit. The player's breath frosts in front of their face, with a soft crunch of snow. The journal notes it.

Runs when: Always

## `tell_restless_hands`

Only a spirit with the restless hands habit. The sound of an item being picked up sounds beside the player, with a puff of dust and nothing taken. The journal notes it.

Runs when: Always

## `tell_door_slammer`

Only a spirit with the door slammer habit. A door slams shut somewhere behind the player, out of sight. The journal notes it.

Runs when: Always

## `tell_meddler`

Only a spirit with the meddler habit. The ground near the player settles with the sound and dust of a block being set down, though none was. The journal notes it.

Runs when: Always

## `tell_thrower`

Only a spirit with the thrower habit. A few flecks of stone and a throwing whoosh pass behind the player, with no hand to be seen. The journal notes it.

Runs when: Always

## `tell_stacker`

Only a spirit with the stacker habit. Two stone bricks stand one on the other a few blocks ahead for 3 s, then vanish. Only this player's client is told of them; nothing changes on the server. The journal notes it.

Runs when: Always (needs two free blocks ahead)

## `tell_knocker`

Only a spirit with the knocker habit. Three slow knocks on wood come from inside a wall near the player. The journal notes it.

Runs when: Always

## `tell_light_eater`

Only a spirit with the light eater habit. A torch within 6 goes out in a curl of smoke and is lit again after 1.5 s. Only this player's client is told; nothing changes on the server. The journal notes it.

Runs when: A torch or soul torch within 6

## `tell_shadow_caster`

Only a spirit with the shadow caster habit. A tall, thin figure of smoke stands about 5 blocks off and thins away, with a faint sigh. The journal notes it.

Runs when: Always

## `tell_blinder`

Only a spirit with the blinder habit. The player's view goes dark for about a second and returns. The journal notes it.

Runs when: Always

## `tell_hearth_killer`

Only a spirit with the hearth killer habit. A lit campfire or furnace within 6 goes out in a puff of smoke and burns again after 2 s. Only this player's client is told; nothing changes on the server. The journal notes it.

Runs when: A lit campfire or furnace within 6

## `tell_corner_lurker`

Only a spirit with the corner lurker habit. Two dark points, a hand apart, hang at head height some blocks away and fade. The journal notes it.

Runs when: Always

## `tell_vengeful`

Only a spirit with the vengeful habit. Four red marks hang in the air a few blocks ahead of the player, with a low moan. The journal notes it.

Runs when: Always

## `tell_hunter`

Only a spirit with the hunter habit. Three heavy footsteps pass behind the player from one side to the other, 0.5 s apart. The journal notes it.

Runs when: Always

## `tell_scratcher`

Only a spirit with the scratcher habit. Three scrapes and sparks sound in the air beside the player, as of claws on a wall. The journal notes it.

Runs when: Always

## `tell_beast_caller`

Only a spirit with the beast caller habit. A wolf's cry sounds from about 24 blocks off, with no wolf near. The journal notes it.

Runs when: Always

## `tell_drowner`

Only a spirit with the drowner habit. Bubbles rise round the player's feet on dry land, with a gurgle. The journal notes it.

Runs when: Always

## `tell_snarler`

Only a spirit with the snarler habit. A low snarl sounds right beside the player's ear. The journal notes it.

Runs when: Always

## `tell_whisperer`

Only a spirit with the whisperer habit. A whisper, too low to make out, sounds close behind the player. The journal notes it.

Runs when: Always

## `tell_liar`

Only a spirit with the liar habit. A villager's yes sounds behind the player, and a villager's no follows it after 0.6 s, with no villager near. The journal notes it.

Runs when: Always

## `tell_face_thief`

Only a spirit with the face thief habit. A small cloud of the player's own head hangs at face height about 2.5 blocks ahead and thins away. The journal notes it.

Runs when: Always

## `tell_illusionist`

Only a spirit with the illusionist habit. A crafting table stands about 4 blocks ahead for 3 s, then vanishes. Only this player's client is told of it; nothing changes on the server. The journal notes it.

Runs when: Always (needs standing room ahead)

## `tell_false_friend`

Only a spirit with the false friend habit. A dog stands about 5 blocks off and watches the player for 4 s, then vanishes. Only this player's client is told of it; no dog exists on the server. The journal notes it.

Runs when: Always (needs standing room ahead)

## `tell_lure`

Only a spirit with the lure habit. A soft light drifts away ahead of the player a little at a time, with a faint chime. The journal notes it.

Runs when: Always
